title: "Bounty Program Closed"
draft: false
date: 2026-11-11
type: "page"
---

Effective immediately the Decred bug bounty program is closed for new
submissions. We aim to have the queue of pending reports completely processed
before the [proposal](https://proposals.decred.org/record/8b1a640) expires at
the end of the year.

We thank all bug hunters for their reports and their contribution toward making
Decred more secure.
